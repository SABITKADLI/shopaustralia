## SHIPPING & RETURNS POLICY

**Last Updated:** December 2025

### 1. Shipping

**Shipping Partners:** [Your Business Name] uses AutoDS, Spocket, and Zendrop for fulfillment. Each has different shipping times and carriers.

**Estimated Delivery Times:**
- Standard Shipping: 5–10 business days (varies by supplier)
- Express Shipping: 2–5 business days (if available)
- Tracking: All orders come with a tracking number via email

**Shipping Address Requirements:**
- Must be a valid Australian address
- We ship to Australia only
- Delivery to PO boxes available (additional cost)
- Residential and business addresses accepted

**Shipping Costs:**
- Free shipping on orders over $100 AUD
- Standard shipping under $100: Calculated at checkout
- Express shipping: Additional fee at checkout

**What Happens if My Order is Lost or Damaged in Transit?**
- Report within 48 hours of delivery (or non-delivery)
- We'll investigate with the carrier
- Replacement or refund within 14 business days

### 2. Returns

**Return Eligibility:**
- Products must be unused and in original condition
- Return window: 14 days from delivery date (ACL requirement)
- Some items may be non-returnable (contact support for specifics)

**How to Initiate a Return:**
1. Log into your account → Orders → Select order → "Request Return"
2. Describe the reason (defective, wrong item, changed mind, etc.)
3. We'll send you a return shipping label
4. Pack securely and drop off at Australia Post

**Return Shipping:**
- Pre-paid label provided (we cover cost)
- Use Australia Post or nominated carrier
- Keep tracking number for proof

**Refund Processing:**
- We receive your return → inspect product (3–5 business days)
- If approved, refund issued to original payment method
- Refunds appear in your account within 5–10 business days

**Non-Returnable Items:**
- Perishable goods
- Items damaged by customer misuse
- Items without original packaging

### 3. Exchanges

- Same process as returns
- Specify desired replacement size/color at checkout
- We ship replacement upon receiving your return

### 4. Damaged or Defective Products

If your product arrives damaged or defective:
1. **Report immediately** (within 48 hours)
2. Provide photos of damage
3. We'll replace or refund without requiring a return

## REFUND POLICY

**Last Updated:** December 2025

### 1. Full Refund Guarantee

We want you to be completely satisfied. If for any reason you're not, you can request a refund within **14 days** of purchase under the Australian Consumer Law.

### 2. Refund Eligibility

**Full Refund if:**
- Product is defective or doesn't match description
- Product doesn't arrive within stated timeframe (+ 7 days grace)
- Changed your mind (within 14 days, unused condition)

**Partial or No Refund if:**
- Item is damaged by customer misuse
- Return requested after 14 days
- Item is counterfeit (rare, but we investigate)

### 3. Refund Process

**Step 1:** Log in and click "Request Refund" on your order  
**Step 2:** Select reason and provide details/photos  
**Step 3:** We review within 2 business days  
**Step 4:** If approved, we send return label  
**Step 5:** You ship back (pre-paid)  
**Step 6:** We inspect and process refund (5–10 business days)

### 4. Refund Timeline

- **Defective items:** Refunded within 3 business days of receipt
- **Changed mind (unused):** Refunded within 7 business days of receipt
- **Refund to account:** 5–10 business days after approval
- **Re-shipment to address:** 7–14 business days

### 5. Refund Method

Refunds are issued to your **original payment method** (credit card, PayPal, etc.). Your bank may take additional time to process (typically 2–5 business days).

### 6. Exceptions

We cannot refund:
- Items where return window (14 days) has passed
- Shipping costs (unless our error)
- Custom or made-to-order items

## COOKIE POLICY

**Last Updated:** December 2025

### 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, phone). They help us remember your preferences, track site usage, and improve your experience.

### 2. Cookies We Use

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `session_id` | Keeps you logged in | Session (1 hour) |
| `cart` | Remembers your shopping cart | 30 days |
| `preference` | Stores your language/theme choice | 1 year |
| `ga_id` | Google Analytics (opt-in) | 2 years |
| `stripe_session` | Payment processing | Session |

### 3. Third-Party Cookies

We use cookies from:
- **Google Analytics:** Tracks site traffic and behavior (opt-in)
- **Shopify:** Processes checkout and orders
- **Stripe:** Handles payment securely

### 4. Managing Cookies

**In Your Browser:**
- Chrome/Edge: Settings → Privacy → Cookies
- Firefox: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
- Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies

You can disable cookies, but some features may not work properly.

**Opt-Out of Analytics:**
- [Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Extension](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)
- Or manage preferences in our website footer ("Manage Cookies")

### 5. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you're a California resident, you can:
- Request what data we collect about you
- Request deletion of your data
- Opt-out of data sales (we don't sell data, but you can opt-out anyway)
